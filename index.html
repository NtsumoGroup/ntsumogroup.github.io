What changes on a busy Saturday

Most of the work in running a diary is repeating the same few steps. That is the part we automate.

TodayWith Ntsumo
Calls and DMs go unanswered while your hands are busy with a client.Clients pick a free slot themselves, at any hour of the day.

How we set it up

  1. We learn how you book today

    A short call about your services, staff, opening hours and the questions clients ask most often.

  2. We build it

    We set up your booking calendar in SimplyBook.me, connect everything with Make.com, and switch on WhatsApp and SMS messages through Twilio.

  3. We test it with your team

    You and your staff make test bookings while we adjust the wording and rules until it sounds like your salon.

  4. You go live, and we stay close

    We watch the first real bookings come in and fix anything that needs it.
