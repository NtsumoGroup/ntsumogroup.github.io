Automation services for appointment-based businesses

We build the booking, messaging and admin systems that salons, doctor's rooms, clinics, gyms and other South African businesses need but rarely have time to set up.

Appointment booking automation

This is where most clients start. Your services or appointment types, staff or practitioners, and opening hours go into one booking system, and people book themselves by WhatsApp, SMS or a link. It suits salons and barbers as much as doctor's rooms, dentists, clinics, gyms and studios, workshops, and professional practices.

  • Online booking page and shared staff or practitioner calendar
  • Instant booking confirmations by WhatsApp or SMS
  • Rules for appointment length, availability and time off

Built with SimplyBook.me, Make.com and Twilio.

Reminders and rebooking

Fewer empty chairs and less back-and-forth. Clients get a reminder before the appointment and can move it with a single reply.

  • Automatic reminders before each appointment
  • One-reply rescheduling that frees the old slot
  • A follow-up message after the visit that invites the next booking

Built with Make.com and Twilio.

Custom workflows and AI assistants

For businesses whose bottleneck is something other than a diary. We map the repetitive task, then automate the parts that do not need a person.

  • Enquiries captured from WhatsApp, email or your website into one place
  • Quotes, invoices and spreadsheet updates sent without retyping
  • An AI assistant that answers common questions from your own prices and hours, and hands over to you when it is unsure

Built with Make.com and language-model APIs, chosen to suit the job.

Questions owners ask

Do my clients or patients need to install anything?

No. They book through WhatsApp, SMS or a booking link that opens in their browser.

Will this replace my receptionist or front desk?

No. It takes over the routine work of finding a slot, confirming it and sending reminders. Anything unusual, or anything that needs judgement, still reaches a person.

Which tools do you use?

Bookings run on SimplyBook.me, the automations run on Make.com, and WhatsApp and SMS messages go out through Twilio. We choose the simplest setup that fits your business.

What does it cost?

The price depends on how many staff or practitioners you have, how many appointment types you offer, and how many messages you send each month. Book a free call and we will give you a clear quote.

Which businesses does this suit?

Any business or practice that runs on booked appointments: salons and barbers, doctor's rooms and clinics, dentists, gyms and studios, workshops, and professional services such as legal or consulting practices. We also build custom automations for other kinds of admin outside of booking.

Can this handle a doctor's room or clinic?

Yes. We set up the booking and reminder flow around your appointment types and practitioners' hours. Messages confirm the time and place only, not clinical details, and the flow is set up to fit how your practice already runs.

How do you handle my clients' or patients' personal information?

We only process what is needed to run bookings and reminders, and we set this up to align with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). For practices handling patient information, the data processing terms for your booking system are agreed with you directly, separately from this website's own privacy policy.

Where are you based?

Limpopo, South Africa. We work remotely with clients across the country.
