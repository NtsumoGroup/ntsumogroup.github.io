GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before you start.

Do I need to be in South Africa?

No. Ntsumo is based in Limpopo, South Africa, and works remotely with clients in South Africa and worldwide. Any required local registrations, taxes and payment arrangements are confirmed based on the actual project and business setup.

Are the prices fixed?

They are introductory starting prices for clearly defined, straightforward work. You receive a written quote for your requirements before paying. Third-party subscriptions, hosting, domains, message charges and advanced integrations are itemised or excluded.

Do you build fully automated WhatsApp bots?

We can scope WhatsApp-related workflows, but the available features and cost depend on the WhatsApp product, provider, approvals and integration requirements. We confirm those details before promising a feature or charging for it.

Can you work with a clinic or healthcare business?

Potentially, but healthcare information needs extra care. We would first review the workflow, data involved, platform terms and privacy requirements, and avoid collecting clinical details unnecessarily.

Will you manage my website forever?

Only if you choose an ongoing support arrangement. The written scope will explain what is maintained, what support is included, and how access and ownership are handed over.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's fine. Explain what takes too much time or causes customer confusion. We'll recommend a small starting point rather than automatically suggesting a large system.
