Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains how Ntsumo (Pty) Ltd handles personal information collected through this website and when you contact us about our services. It is a general website privacy notice, not a substitute for project-specific data-processing terms.

Who is responsible?

Ntsumo (Pty) Ltd, based in Limpopo, South Africa, is responsible for enquiries submitted through this website. Contact us at sibisifrank3@gmail.com.

What information may be collected?

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address or WhatsApp number, country, selected service and the message you provide. The hosting provider may also process technical information needed to serve and secure the website.

Why do we use it?

We use enquiry information to respond to you, assess whether we can help, prepare a quote and maintain reasonable business records. We do not sell enquiry information.

Service providers

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. If the contact form is connected, submissions may be processed by Formspree as configured in the form. We may use email and communication providers to reply. Their own terms and privacy policies also apply.

International transfers

Because Ntsumo may work with customers in different countries and use online service providers, information may be processed outside the country where you are located. We will take steps required by applicable law for the information and services involved.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, manage a business relationship and meet legal or recordkeeping obligations. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction or deletion of personal information, or raise a concern about its handling. Contact sibisifrank3@gmail.com. South African data subjects may also contact the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Cookies and analytics

We do not intentionally run advertising trackers or analytics tools on this version of the website. The hosting provider may process technical logs as part of normal service operation.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our practices change. The date at the top will be revised when we do.
