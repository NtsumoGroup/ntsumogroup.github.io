Who we are

This website is run by Ntsumo (Pty) Ltd, Limpopo, South Africa. We are the responsible party for personal information collected here under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You can reach us at sibisifrank3@gmail.com.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we collect the details you type in: your name, business name, WhatsApp number or email, and your message. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss work with you. We do not sell your information.

Who processes it for us

Contact form submissions are handled by Formspree, which passes them to us. GitHub hosts the website.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not currently use cookies or analytics tools.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as needed to respond and to keep reasonable business records, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can object to how we use it. Email us to do so. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
