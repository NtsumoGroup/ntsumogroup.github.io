Technology should make work simpler—not add another layer of work.

Ntsumo brings a software engineering mindset to the everyday problems small businesses face: getting enquiries, managing bookings and keeping information organised.

Ntsumo is a technology business based in Limpopo, South Africa. It was founded by Ntsako Sibisi, a full-stack developer with experience building software and digital products.

We apply that engineering discipline to smaller, practical projects: a website that answers customer questions, a booking journey that is easier to follow, or a workflow that reduces repetitive admin.

Where we work

We are based in Limpopo, South Africa, and can deliver suitable projects remotely to businesses in South Africa and Zimbabwe. We are building our Harare customer base and do not currently claim to have a local office or partner there.

Typical tools include web technologies, calendars, forms and workflow automation platforms. The specific stack is chosen to fit the project rather than force every client onto the same system.
