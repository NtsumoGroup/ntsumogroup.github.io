About Ntsumo

Ntsumo (Pty) Ltd is a small technology company based in Limpopo, South Africa. It was founded by Ntsako Sibisi.

Ntsako is a full stack engineer who builds software in Java, C# and Android, and delivers large enterprise systems in his day-to-day work. Ntsumo brings that engineering discipline to small businesses that do not have an IT department: booking, messaging and admin that simply works.
